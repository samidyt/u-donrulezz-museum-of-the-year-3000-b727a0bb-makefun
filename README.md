## The entry

One line: the museum label a museum in the year 3000 places next to an
ordinary object from today — a group chat.

> Group Chat, c. 2025. A room with no walls where 47 people talked at once about nothing, and called it friendship.

113 characters, one line, plain English. The joke is the year-3000
misreading: future curators treat a group chat as a physical room that
somehow held 47 people, talking all at once about nothing — and the
people in it called that friendship.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the label to `/output/entry.txt` (or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set), after a self-check that the
label passes the gate: one line, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
